## The lesson-notes incident

The first version of E let a lesson compile call `write_page` on existing slugs and stamped the
compile pipeline's book/chapter citation into `sources`. Live, it replaced a tutor-written `solid`
page (two Rust Book URLs) with a `draft` citing `lesson-notes` and `chapter 1`, and cut its body by
about 60%. `withLessonRules` in `ingest.ts` now refuses writes to pages that existed before the
compile (stubs excepted) and keeps only source URLs the turn actually used. The user's vault was
repaired through Engram from the tutor's original `write_page` inputs in the saved threads. Keep
the four real-Engram tests in `tests/lessonNotes.test.ts`; they reproduce the incident.
