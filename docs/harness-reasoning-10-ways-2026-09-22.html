03

Receipt

an itemized bill of what was done for you

Nothing shows while the tutor works. When the turn ends, it “prints” a receipt beside the message: every action as a line item, plus totals for what actually matters, like relevant sources found (0), pages written, claims left unverified. The honest bottom line is the point: it adds up what the turn produced.

04

Provenance ink

no tool list at all

Tool calls vanish. Instead every sentence is underlined by where it came from: solid green if it's from a vault page, blue if it's from a web source you can click, dashed amber if it's from the model's memory. Hovering shows which call supplied it. A bar under the message shows the mix. This is the most direct answer to “it just relies on its training data”: you'd see it happening, sentence by sentence.

06

Self-interview

each tool call as a question the tutor asked itself

Each call is rewritten as the question behind it, with the answer it got: “Do I know this learner? No record.” The chain ends in a verdict box that states the decision and why. You see the tutor's reasoning as a Socratic chain in plain language, not as tool names.

08

The crew

the harness's parts talk as characters

The harness's subsystems appear as a small backstage crew that reports in: Registrar (your record), Librarian (sources and search), Scribe (the vault) and Examiner (grading). They speak in first person, and they can object. The Librarian warning that the tutor is about to teach from memory is the harness's standards speaking up.

09

Graph pulse

the tool calls happen on your knowledge graph

A small copy of your knowledge graph sits beside the message. Tool calls act on it as they happen: a read makes a node glow, a search sweeps across a region, a write adds a new node that grows in, and recorded evidence fills a node's mastery ring. Research that comes back empty shows as a dashed ghost node outside the graph. You see the vault growing, or not growing, as you learn.

Mixing them

  • 4 + 10: provenance ink for studying, the flight recorder behind a developer toggle. One is for trust, the other for debugging, with little overlap.
  • 6 + 3: self-interview while the turn runs, then a receipt when it ends. Explains the reasoning, then states the outcome.
  • 2 collapsed: the subway line shrunk to a single colored bar under each message, the smallest step up from today's checkmarks.
